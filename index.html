How to Play

  • Drag from the comet to aim — the drag sets the angle. Set the power with the slider or the arrow keys, then release to launch (or press the Launch button, or Space). A plain click never launches — every shot is a decision.
  • The preview shows only the first second of flight. There is no prediction — judge the shot yourself, then fly it.
  • Mid-flight, hold the mouse / finger to burn thrust toward it, or hold WASD / arrow keys to steer. Thrust burns fuel — the gold bar in the HUD. Fly through gold orbs to refill fuel and bank +50 bonus each. Teal boost pads refill +50 fuel and kick you faster along your flight — the fast line always runs close to something dangerous.
  • Planets bend your flight with gravity. Use the bend, don't fight it. Skim close past a planet for a slingshot flash — time briefly slows so you can watch the bend — each slingshot pays a +100 gravity-assist bonus.
  • Collect every magenta target before you run out of shots. Chain two stars on one flight with a good burn — par rewards flying, not just aiming.
  • Crashing into a planet or flying out of bounds loses the shot. Black holes pull hard and swallow comets — arc around them, never through them.
  • Moving planets orbit their centre (the faint ring shows the path). Time your launch for when the bend lines up, then burn to chase the stars as the bend moves.
  • Portals come in matching pairs — fly into one and you exit its twin at the same speed. Shielded targets (white ring) take two hits: the first cracks the shield, the second collects the star.
  • Press Space mid-flight (or the Skip button) to fast-forward a long drift.
  • Beat par for 3 stars. Finish a level to unlock the next one.
  • Ship upgrades unlock with campaign stars — Ion Thrusters (+25% thrust power) at 15 stars, Big Tank (+30% fuel) at 30 stars. Toggle them in Level Select; equipped upgrades show in the HUD.
  • Achievements track your career — first launch, one-shot clears, portal hops, slingshots and more. Check them from the main menu.
  • Audio Settings in the menu (and the pause menu): pick a music style — Nebula, Pulse or Deep Field — toggle sound effects or music, and turn on reduce flashing to skip the red crash flash.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: drag to aim (angle), release to launch · hold mid-flight to steer with thrust
  • Power slider · arrow keys: angle (0.1° steps) and power · hold Shift for fine aim
  • WASD / arrows mid-flight: steer with thrust · Space mid-flight: fast-forward · Space while aiming: launch
  • Pinch to zoom on touch, or the mouse wheel · the HUD − / + buttons and the + / − keys zoom too
  • Enter confirms on menus (Play, Next, Retry, Resume) · arrow keys move between level buttons · Esc closes this panel
  • R: retry level · P: pause · M: mute · volume slider in the pause menu
