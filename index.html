How to Play

  • Drag back from the comet and release to launch it — like a slingshot. Or tap / click anywhere to launch with the current aim.
  • Planets bend your flight with gravity. Use the bend, don't fight it.
  • Collect every magenta target before you run out of shots.
  • Crashing into a planet or flying out of bounds loses the shot.
  • Black holes pull hard and swallow comets that cross their event horizon — arc around them, never through them.
  • Skim close past a planet for a slingshot flash — time briefly slows so you can watch the bend — and watch the shockwave ring when you collect a star.
  • Moving planets orbit their centre (the faint ring shows the path). The trajectory preview follows them — time your launch for when the bend lines up.
  • Portals come in matching pairs — fly into one and you exit its twin at the same speed. Shielded targets (white ring) take two hits: the first cracks the shield, the second collects the star. Bounce pads (lime) reflect the comet like a mirror.
  • Moving targets drift on small orbits (the faint ring shows the path). Lead your shot so the comet meets the target, not where it was.
  • Time Attack clears levels 1–5 against one 4:00 clock — every second counts, and the best full clear is saved.
  • Zen Sandbox is a free orbit playground — unlimited shots, no fail state, and collected stars drift back onto the field. Your best session is saved.
  • Daily Orbit is one fresh puzzle every day — the same level for everyone. Endless Orbit keeps dealing harder levels until you run out of shots.
  • Type a seed (or open a ?seed= link) to replay the exact same generated level as a friend.
  • Beat par for 3 stars. Finish a level to unlock the next one.
  • Achievements track your career — first launch, one-shot clears, portal hops, slingshots and more. Check them from the main menu.

Controls

  • Mouse / touch: drag to aim, release to launch · tap / click to launch with the current aim
  • Pinch to zoom on touch, or the mouse wheel · the HUD − / + buttons and the + / − keys zoom too
  • Hold Shift while aiming for fine adjustments (or Shift + arrow keys)
  • Arrow keys: adjust angle and power · Space or Enter: launch
  • Enter confirms on menus (Play, Next, Retry, Resume) · arrow keys move between level buttons · Esc closes this panel
  • R: retry level · P: pause · M: mute · volume slider in the pause menu
